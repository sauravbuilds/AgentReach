# Discussion Finder

**Live app: https://agentreach-one.vercel.app/**

Type a topic, or describe the kind of discussion you want, and get links to recent Reddit conversations with a short description of each.

## How to use it

1. Type a topic or description, for example `people comparing budgeting apps` or `NRE vs NRO account`.
2. Optionally add how far back to look: `last 7 days`, `past 2 weeks`, `last 45 days` (maximum 90, default 90).
3. Press **Search**, then click a result to open the post on Reddit.
4. Your searches are saved in the sidebar. Click one to reopen it, double-click to rename it, or use the **x** to delete it.

## India and NRI topics

Short terms like **RNOR**, **NRE**, **NRO**, **OCI** or **FEMA** are easy to confuse with unrelated words. Discussion Finder recognises common NRI, India tax and banking terms, searches for the right topic, drops look-alike posts and shows a plain-English meaning of the term above the results.

## Good to know

- **Reddit only for now.** X / Twitter is coming later.
- **Very recent posts may be missing at busy moments**, because Reddit limits how often it can be asked for the latest ones. Results show "via" which sources answered.
- **Your history stays in your browser.** There is no login, so clearing browser data or switching device starts a fresh history.
- **Searches are rate-limited** to keep the service available for everyone. Repeating a recent search is instant.
- **Descriptions are unverified excerpts** and Reddit comments are opinions, not advice. For tax, legal or money decisions, check with a qualified professional.
