# Discussion Finder

Find what people are actually saying about a topic. Type a topic, or describe the kind of discussion you are looking for, and get links to recent Reddit conversations with a short description of each.

## How to use it

1. Type a topic or a description in the box, for example:
   - `people comparing budgeting apps`
   - `NRE vs NRO account`
   - `moving back to India, what happens to my taxes`
2. Optionally say how far back to look: `last 7 days`, `past 2 weeks`, `last 45 days`. The maximum is 90 days, and the default is 90.
3. Press **Search**. Each result shows the post title, the subreddit, the date, a short excerpt and the link. Click a card to open the post on Reddit.
4. Your searches are saved in the sidebar. Click one to reopen it, double-click to rename it, or use the **x** to delete it.

## Searching India and NRI topics

Short terms like **RNOR**, **NRE**, **NRO**, **OCI**, **FEMA** or **15CA** are easy to confuse with unrelated words. Discussion Finder recognises common NRI and India tax and banking terms, searches for the right topic and drops posts that only look similar. When it recognises a term, a short plain-English meaning appears above the results so you can see what was searched.

Terms it knows include: RNOR, ROR, NRI, OCI, PIO, NRE, NRO, FCNR, FEMA, DTAA, TRC and Form 10F, Form 15CA/15CB, Section 195 TDS, LRS, TCS on remittances, AIS and Form 26AS, deemed residency, the 182-day rule, FATCA, FBAR, PFIC, Schedule FA, Form 67, GIFT City, PIS accounts and repatriation.

## Good to know

- **Reddit only for now.** X / Twitter is being built and will be added later.
- **Results can lag behind Reddit.** The search source updates with a delay, so the newest posts may not show up yet.
- **Your history is private to your browser.** There is no account or login. Chats are tied to the browser you use, so clearing your browser data or switching to another device starts a fresh history.
- **Searches are limited** so the service stays available for everyone: a handful per minute and a daily cap per person. Repeating a search you or someone else just made is instant and does not count.
- **Descriptions are excerpts.** They come from the post text and are not verified. For tax, legal or money decisions, read the full discussion and check with a qualified professional. Reddit comments are opinions, not advice.

## Something not working?

- *"The search service is busy"*: it is rate-limited at peak times. Wait a minute and try again.
- *No results*: try a longer date range, different words, or describe the situation instead of using only an acronym.
- *History missing*: it lives in your browser, so it is gone if you cleared site data or changed browser.
